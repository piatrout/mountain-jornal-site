The Comici–Dimai was one of the first routes up the north face of the Cima Grande and is still a benchmark for steep Dolomite climbing.

## Approach

From the Auronzo hut, it is a short walk around to the north side. The face stays in shade all day, so bring warm layers even in summer.

## The climbing

The lower third is steep and sustained, with many old pegs. Above, the angle eases into long grooves and chimneys where route-finding becomes the main challenge.

## Descent

The south-side descent involves several rappels and downclimbing; doing it in daylight makes a big difference.

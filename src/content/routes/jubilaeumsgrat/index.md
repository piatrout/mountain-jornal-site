The Jubiläumsgrat links the Zugspitze with the Alpspitze along roughly eight kilometres of limestone ridge. Most of it is scrambling, with a handful of short climbing steps and a few cabled sections.

## Approach

We took the first cable car to the Zugspitze to save the morning and started along the ridge before the crowds arrived.

## The ridge

The rock is solid on the crest and loose on the flanks. Route-finding matters more than climbing ability: stay on the crest unless the line clearly drops off it.

## Descent

From the Alpspitze we descended via the ferrata to the Osterfelderkopf and took the last cable car down.

The South Ridge is one of the best-known granite climbs in central Switzerland: steady climbing on rough, clean rock with a hut approach that keeps the day manageable.

## Approach

From the Salbithütte, a short walk leads to the foot of the ridge. Start early; the route is popular on summer weekends.

## The climbing

Most pitches sit in the IV range, with a couple of harder moves around V. Protection is good with a standard rack of cams.

## Descent

Several rappels and a careful scramble lead back to the hut path.
